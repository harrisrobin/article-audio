# Article Audio

Article Audio turns supplied articles into faithful audio readings and maintains the setup needed to deliver them.

## Language

**Release**:
A published version of the portable Article Audio package. Each reusable Bot template refers to a specific release.

**Template bundle**:
The complete public Bot description, skills, shared setup guidance, and release reference prepared for sharing. It excludes an owner's credentials, preferences, setup status, and recordings.

**Published template**:
The shared Bot configuration that Grok has saved and made available for others to add. Preparing a template bundle does not establish that this configuration has been published.

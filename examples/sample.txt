The best tools leave room for your attention.

Imagine finishing a long day with three interesting articles still waiting in your bookmarks. Reading each one means another stretch in front of a screen. Listening gives those ideas a different place in the day: a walk, a train journey, or twenty quiet minutes making dinner.

A good narration keeps the author's argument intact. The examples still matter. So do the qualifications, the awkward questions, and the details that refuse to fit into a tidy summary. A calm voice can make that complexity easier to follow without making it disappear.

That is the purpose of this small experiment: take words worth reading, give them space to be heard, and return a recording that is easy to keep.
